What's inside

Fifteen modules. Switch on only what you need.

Each module works standalone or together. Turn on what your business needs today, and add more the moment you need it.

01

Job cards and workshop

Manage service jobs from intake to delivery with a full status workflow and audit trail.

  • Job card creation, search, filter and print
  • Complaint registration and prioritisation
  • Technician and bay assignment with availability
  • QC approval, customer sign-off and delivery handover
See it in action
02

Live vehicle tracking

Customers check real-time status without a login, a call, or an account.

  • Public, no-login QR/link tracking page
  • Real-time job status by vehicle registration or job number
  • Shareable link customers can send to anyone
  • Offers and promo banners shown right on the tracking page
See it in action
03

Invoicing and billing

Turn closed jobs into invoices without re-entering a single line item.

  • Auto-generate invoices from closed job cards
  • Labour, parts and tax line items
  • PDF generation, print and payment status
  • Posts straight to the general ledger
See it in action
04

Inventory and warranties

Know what's on the shelf, and back every part you sell with a printable warranty.

  • Parts catalog and stock levels
  • Reserve, consume and return tracking
  • Low-stock and availability checks
  • Per-part warranty records with a printable Warranty Card
See it in action
05

Payroll and HR

From hire to payslip, in one system.

  • Salary structures and statutory deductions
  • Payroll runs and payslip generation
  • Recruitment/ATS with interview scheduling
  • Onboarding checklists and performance reviews
See it in action
06

Accounting

Close the loop from operations to the general ledger.

  • Auto-posting from invoices and payroll
  • Trial balance
  • Unposted-invoice tracking
