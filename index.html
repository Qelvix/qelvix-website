What's inside

Every module your operation needs

Each module works standalone or together — turn on what your business needs today, add more as you grow.

🛠️

Job Card & Workshop Management

Manage service jobs from intake to delivery with a full status workflow and audit trail.

  • Job card creation, search, filter & print
  • Complaint registration & prioritisation
  • Technician & bay assignment with availability
  • Labour tracking — estimated vs. actual hours
  • QC approval and customer sign-off on closure
See it in action arrow_forward
🚗

Customer & Vehicle Tracking

Give customers visibility without giving them a login.

  • Customer & vehicle master records
  • Ownership validation & service history
  • Public no-login QR tracking portal
  • Real-time job status for customers
See it in action arrow_forward
🧾

Invoicing & Billing

Turn closed jobs into invoices without re-entering a single line item.

  • Auto-generate invoices from closed job cards
  • Labour, parts & tax line items
  • PDF generation & print
  • Invoice tracking & payment status
See it in action arrow_forward
📦

Inventory & Stock

Know what's on the shelf before you promise it to a customer.

  • Parts catalog & stock levels
  • Reserve, consume & return tracking
  • Stock adjustments with audit history
  • Low-stock and availability checks
See it in action arrow_forward
👥

Payroll & HR

From hire to payslip, in one system.

  • Salary structures & statutory deductions
  • Payroll runs & payslip generation
  • Recruitment/ATS with interview scheduling
  • Onboarding checklists & performance reviews
  • Overtime requests & compensatory leave
See it in action arrow_forward
📱

Employee Self-Service Portal

Give every employee a mobile-friendly home for their own HR needs.

  • Payslips, leave requests & balances
  • Onboarding checklists for new hires
  • Performance review tracking
  • Installable as a PWA on any phone
See it in action arrow_forward
📊

Accounting & Reporting

Close the loop from operations to the general ledger.

  • Chart of accounts & journal entry engine
  • Auto-posting from invoices
  • Ledger & trial balance
  • Revenue, technician & utilisation KPIs
  • Fixed asset register with depreciation
See it in action arrow_forward
🚚

Delivery & Scheduling

Coordinate the last mile alongside the shop floor.

  • Delivery scheduling & dispatch log
  • Workshop capacity & scheduling dashboard
  • Real-time work allocation across bays
See it in action arrow_forward
🔒

Security & Multi-Tenancy

Enterprise-grade controls from day one.

  • Role-based access on every endpoint
  • Company-scoped data isolation (Hibernate filters)
  • Secure token handling & brute-force protection
  • Full audit trail on every state change
See it in action arrow_forward
⚙️

Platform & Operations

Built to run reliably, not just to demo well.

  • Containerized backend & frontend
  • Redis-backed caching & rate limiting
  • CI/CD pipelines with coverage gates
  • Prometheus metrics & structured logging

Why Qelvix

Built as a platform, not a point solution

Qelvix is a generic multi-business ERP — every module is configurable to your business type rather than hardcoded to one industry.

🧩

Configurable by design

Terminology and workflows adapt per business — job card, technician, and bay are generic patterns, not fixed labels.

🏢

Multi-tenant ready

Data is scoped per company at the database layer, so one deployment can safely serve many businesses.

🔐

Secure by default

Role-based permissions, audit trails, and hardened auth are built into the platform, not bolted on later.

📈

Grows with you

Start with one module — job cards, or payroll — and turn on more as your operation expands.
